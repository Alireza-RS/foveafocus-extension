# FoveaFocus: privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

FoveaFocus is a browser extension that shows the text of a web page, a PDF or a text file one word at a time.

## What it handles

The text of the page, PDF or file that is open in the tab in which a reading is asked for. This text is read inside the browser, on the device, for one purpose only: to show it in the reader. A PDF is fetched once more from its own address, so that the viewer of the extension can show it.

## What it keeps

The settings of the reader, and whether its bar is shown, in the browser's storage for extensions on the device. Nothing of what is read is kept.

## What it sends

Nothing. The extension has no server and no account. It makes no request to any third party, uses no analytics and shows no advertising. No data is sold, shared or passed on to anyone.

## Limited use

The use of the information that the extension handles complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removal

Removing the extension from the browser removes its settings with it.

## Contact

Questions can be put as an issue at https://github.com/Alireza-RS/foveafocus-extension/issues, or through the support tab of the extension's page in the Chrome Web Store.
